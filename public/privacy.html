DownStock — Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What DownStock accesses

DownStock reads your store's products, collections, and inventory levels, and reorders products within collections you enable. It does not read or store customer data (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or orders) — its data model is entirely product/collection scoped.

What we store

  • Your shop domain and Shopify access token, to authenticate API requests
  • Which collections you've enabled, and their compatibility status
  • Product sellability state (available / sold out), so we can detect changes
  • A record of products DownStock has moved, so it can restore them accurately
  • An activity log of moves and restores, shown in your DownStock dashboard

Data retention and deletion

If you uninstall DownStock, we stop processing your store immediately and delete operational data. Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks (customer data request, customer redact, shop redact) are fully supported; since we never store customer data, the customer-related requests require no action. Full shop data is permanently deleted upon receiving the shop redact webhook.
